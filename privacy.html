1) Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how ToteKit (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use the ToteKit mobile app. If you have questions, contact us at info@hudsonsolution.sk.

2) Data we collect

We aim to collect the minimum data needed to operate the App. Depending on the features you use, we may process:

  • App content you provide: e.g., items, notes, or settings you create in the App.
  • Device and usage data (if applicable): basic technical information such as device model, OS version, app version, and crash logs.
  • Push notification tokens (if you enable notifications): a device token used to deliver notifications.

3) Data we do NOT sell

We do not sell your personal information.

4) How we use information

  • To provide and maintain the App’s core functionality.
  • To improve reliability and performance (e.g., diagnosing crashes).
  • To send notifications you request (if you enable them).
  • To comply with legal obligations where required.

5) Permissions

The App may request device permissions (for example: notifications, photos, camera). You can manage permissions anytime in iOS Settings. If you deny a permission, some features may not work.

6) Push notifications

If you enable push notifications, Apple Push Notification service (APNs) is used to deliver notifications. We use a device token to route notifications to your device. You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings.

7) Analytics and crash reporting (if applicable)

If we use analytics or crash reporting tools, they may collect limited device and usage information (e.g., crash traces, performance metrics). We use this information to improve stability and user experience.

If you do not use analytics/crash reporting at all, remove this section.

8) Data storage and retention

We retain information only as long as necessary to provide the App and for legitimate business or legal purposes. If the App stores data locally on your device, deleting the App typically removes local data from that device.

9) Sharing

We may share limited information with:

  • Service providers that help operate the App (e.g., crash reporting). They process data on our behalf.
  • Legal requirements if we must comply with law, regulation, or valid legal process.

10) Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data. Contact us at info@hudsonsolution.sk to request assistance.

11) Children’s privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12) Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the “Last updated” date above.

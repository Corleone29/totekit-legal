FAQ

  • I don’t receive notifications. Check iOS Settings → Notifications → ToteKit, and ensure notifications are enabled.
  • The app icon/logo looks wrong. Make sure you installed the build (TestFlight or device build), not just Expo Go.
  • The app crashes. Please email us a brief description of what you did right before it happened.
  • How do I delete my data? Email us with your request. If your data is stored only locally, deleting the app removes it from your device.

Response times

We typically respond within 2–3 business days. During early beta testing, response times may vary.
